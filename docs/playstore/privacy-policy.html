Privacy Policy

Benkyo Studio · Last updated: 21 October 2026

The short version: Benkyo Studio collects no personal data. It has no internet permission, so it technically cannot send anything anywhere.

1. Who we are

Benkyo Studio is published by Hive Pulse Interactive ("we"). It is an open source study app for the Japanese language.

  • App name: Benkyo Studio
  • Android package id: com.hivepulse.benkyo
  • Source code: github.com/ghettogecko0225-code/Japanese-Sensei-AI

2. Data we collect

None.

The Android version of Benkyo Studio requests only the RECORD_AUDIO permission and explicitly does not request the INTERNET permission. There is no code in the app that can open a network connection.

That means the app cannot and does not:

  • create an account or ask you to sign in,
  • collect your name, email address, phone number or device identifiers,
  • run analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs,
  • show ads or sponsored content,
  • send notifications or push messages,
  • collect your location,
  • collect your contacts, photos, files or clipboard,
  • share anything with us or with any third party.

There are no third-party analytics, advertising or attribution services in the app. There are no tracking pixels, no SDKs and no hidden network calls.

3. What stays on your device

Everything the app needs is stored in the app's own private storage on your device:

WhatWhyWhere
Study progress, review history, scores So the app can build your daily plan and show your progress benkyo.db in the app's private storage
Your settings (theme, language, voice, daily goal) So the app remembers how you like to study The same private storage
A small diagnostic log To help debug speech-recognition problems on your own device asr_log.txt in the app's private storage

This data is never uploaded. It is visible only to the app itself and to anyone with physical access to your unlocked device.

4. Microphone

The app asks for the microphone only when you tap a button that records audio (for example Speak or Shadowing).

  • The audio is captured in memory, processed by the on-device speech model, and discarded.
  • Voice recordings are not saved as files and are not transmitted.
  • The app starts recording only after you tap the record button and stops when the capture finishes or you leave the screen.

5. Removing your data

  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything, including your study history and settings, because all of it lives in the app's private storage.
  • To clear it without uninstalling: Android Settings → Apps → Benkyo Studio → Storage → Clear data.

6. Children

Benkyo Studio is a general-audience study app and is not directed at children under 13. It is not part of Google Play's Family Program and does not collect any information from anyone, including children.
